# H4 EnergyPilot — Commercial strategy

## Suggested plan tiers (hypotheses, not validated prices)
- Community: source code and local evaluation.
- Solo: one individual workspace, reasonable usage quotas.
- Team: multi-user workspace, collaboration and exports.
- Enterprise: OIDC, audit retention, support SLAs and data residency choices.

## KPIs to validate
Trial-to-activated rate, time-to-first-result, active customer organizations, net revenue retention, support costs, infrastructure cost per transaction, error budget and customer satisfaction.

## Launch gate
Interviews, formal pilot acceptance criteria, security/legal reviews, published terms and privacy policies, payment reconciliation, performance/uptime measurements and a production runbook. No pricing or growth claims without market evidence.
